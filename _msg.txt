Fix pan tearing; add procedural ambience

Tearing
- The adaptive scaler had no cooldown. Changing the render size destroys and
  recreates ~37 GL objects (every FBO, texture and depth buffer) and forces TAA
  to rebuild its history, so the change frame is itself very expensive. Panning
  raises frame time on its own, that triggered a drop, the drop cost pushed the
  average higher still, and the scaler cascaded down several steps in a row.
  That cascade is what read as tearing. A 30-frame (about 0.5 s) cooldown now
  separates the steps so each one can be measured before another is applied.
- buildTargets also short-circuits when the dimensions are unchanged, so a
  resize event or a sub-pixel scale step no longer reallocates everything and
  wipes the temporal history for nothing.

Ambience (site/src/audio.js, fully synthesised - no sample files)
- Room tone: brown noise through a lowpass, drifting under two slow LFOs.
- Fluorescent hum: 50 Hz and its odd harmonics through bandpasses, with a slow
  amplitude drift and occasional flicker dips like a failing tube.
- Water drips: short falling sines, randomly panned and spaced, plus occasional
  double-drips, all fed into a synthesised convolution reverb so they read as a
  large tiled room rather than clicks.
- Hysteria: a barely-there cluster of detuned high voices that swells in and
  recedes every 16-50 s, lowpassed and drenched in reverb so it stays distant.
- Audio cannot autoplay, so it starts on the first click or key press. Volume is
  ?vol= (default 0.55), with [ and ] to adjust and M to mute.
- Suspends when the tab is hidden.
